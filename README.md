**Blinkit Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard**

**Project Overview**
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, profitability, delivery efficiency, and customer payment behavior. The dashboard helps identify top-selling products, category-wise performance, city-wise sales distribution, and operational efficiency through interactive visualizations.

**Business Objective**
Analyze Blinkit's sales transactions to identify revenue trends, profitable categories, customer payment preferences, and delivery performance across different cities.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Orders
Total Quantity Sold
Average Delivery Time
Total Cost

**Dashboard Visualizations**
1. Daily Sales Performance
Analyzes sales trends over time and identifies sales fluctuations.

2. Sales by Category
Compares category-wise revenue contribution.

3. City-wise Sales & Profit Distribution
Interactive map showing geographical business performance.

4. Payment Mode Distribution
Shows customer payment preferences among Cash, UPI, and Card transactions.

5. Top Selling Products
Highlights products generating the highest sales.

**Key Business Insights**
Milk, Chips, Banana, and Juice are among the highest-selling products.
Dairy and Fruits & Vegetables categories contribute significantly to revenue.
Digital payment methods (UPI and Card) are widely preferred.
Sales performance varies across cities, indicating regional business opportunities.
Delivery efficiency plays an important role in customer satisfaction.
